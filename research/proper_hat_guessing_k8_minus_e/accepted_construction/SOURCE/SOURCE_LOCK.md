# Source lock and literature scope

## Primary source

Sam Adriaensen, Peter Bentley, Anurag Bishnoi, Wouter Cames van Batenburg,
Michael Kreiger, Lars van der Kuil, Saptarshi Mandal, Anurag Ramachandran, and
James Tuite, *Hat guessing with proper colorings*, arXiv:2603.04909v4,
24 August 2026.

- Canonical identifier: `arXiv:2603.04909v4`
- Abstract page: `https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.04909`
- HTML: `https://arxiv.org/html/2603.04909v4`
- Used result: Lemma 1.2, `HG_P(G) <= |V(G)| + chi(G) - 1`.
- Open problem: Problem 7.4 asks for `HG_P(K_n-e)` and states the two-value
  frontier `{2n-3,2n-2}`.

The source is cited for the upper bound and problem provenance only. The
fourteen-colour lower construction in this package is new to this package.
