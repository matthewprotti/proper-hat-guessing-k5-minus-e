## 1. The proposed construction works

The previous quota-flow pilot only certified the equal-twin sector.
Its frozen two omitted colors per tail were read as data, and the distinct
residual graph was rebuilt. With the simple smaller-first orientation,
a matching of all 42,570 distinct residual orbits exists.

Seven further diagonal-safe selections were generated by integral quota
flows. For each of the eight selections (one inherited plus seven fresh),
the sorted orientation and 16 explicitly specified randomized orientations
were tested. All 136 have complete distinct matchings of size 42,570.

Every matching was checked for genuine incidence and endpoint uniqueness.
A separate standard-library checker reconstructed normalization by
enumerating invertible projective matrices, rather than importing the
builder's determinant formula or trusting SciPy. All 136 certificates
passed. These are finite facts, not a universal compatibility theorem.

The initial eight-flow construction run took about 2.6 seconds in the
recorded environment, excluding interpreter startup. This is an observed
research-runtime datum, not a production performance benchmark.

## 2. A stronger result: no search over 2^380 orientations

Fix the omitted pairs of the inherited diagonal-safe pilot. Select 380
explicit normalized tail IDs. Allow both pair orientations independently
on these tails and fix the smaller-first orientation on the other 610.

An orientation flip does not change a diagonal residual. For a distinct
twin-color cell, the union of the residual sets for the two orientations
is the entire off-diagonal board. Therefore every mutable tail adds 13
vertices to the distinct residual envelope.

The envelope has 42,570+13*380=47,510 left orbits and 47,520 possible right
views. A left-saturating matching is exhibited. It combines with the
unchanged 5,940-row diagonal matching to give ONE common clique policy.

This one policy wins for all 2^380 permitted equivariant twin-rule pairs.
The verification work is not exponential: each normalized proper coloring
is checked against its one or two permitted local twin orientations, for
87,680 total coloring/orientation contexts. There are zero failures.

The baseline sorted-orientation correct-guess histogram on 63,360 normalized
proper colorings is:
1 correct: 57,802; 2 correct: 5,176; 3 correct: 382.
This is a new strategy, not the histogram of the previously accepted
index-affine K8 strategy.

## 3. Optimality has a precise scope

With 381 mutable whole normalized tail orbits, the envelope would contain
47,523 distinct residuals but have only 47,520 right views. One fixed
clique policy cannot cover it, because each labeled clique view and its
chosen guess can cover at most one full-coloring orbit.

Thus the certificate attains the exact maximum 380 within this shared
clique-policy, independent whole-orbit pair-reversal model. It is NOT a
bound on all possible strategy families or on the freedom available when
the clique functions may be recomputed.

## 4. General mathematics versus finite evidence

The diagonal quota-flow existence argument works for every n>=3 on the
full unquotiented graph. Residual-cycle augmentation preserves that
guarantee and connects integral feasible flows. Pair reversals preserve
a fixed equal-sector matching.

The orientation-envelope lemma is also general. It gives a sufficient and
necessary common-completion matching condition for a fixed pair family,
and the capacity bound
f<=floor((n-3)M/(2n-3)).
It does not itself prove an envelope matching exists.

The existence of a capacity-optimal envelope is certified here only for
the stated K8 normalized geometry. A uniform Hall/expansion theorem
for a specified general-n construction remains open.

## 5. A more focused next research question

The next worthwhile target is to explain the near-capacity envelope matching:
which properties of a diagonal-safe flow and a mutable-tail set guarantee
Hall for their union residual? The present exact certificate supplies a
sharp base case and a necessary capacity bound.

Repeating arbitrary single-rule searches would add less than studying
the obstruction to a COMMON completion. The diagonal invariant and
envelope provide two explicit interfaces for proof and implementation.

## 6. Commercial interpretation, not a product claim

The reusable idea is to certify a family of permitted upstream choices
against one fixed downstream policy, rather than certify each variant
from scratch. This can motivate a bounded proof-carrying allocation demo
inside Monitor: identify an explicit allowed-change set, a certificate
covering that set, and a checker that rejects out-of-scope changes.

The hat-guessing theorem does not establish the semantics, performance,
market demand, or regulatory adequacy of such a workflow. Those remain
separate validation tasks. No production code or customer data was used.
