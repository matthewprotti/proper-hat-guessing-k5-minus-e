# The proper hat-guessing number of `K6-e`

> **Public research disclosure v0.1 - 3 September 2026**  
> **Status:** theorem proved; adversarial mathematical review completed; conventional peer review pending.

## Result

Let `K6-e` be the complete graph on six vertices with one edge removed. Then

\[
\boxed{\mathrm{HG}_{P}(K_6-e)=10}.
\]

The upper bound is the general inequality

\[
\mathrm{HG}_{P}(G)\le |V(G)|+\chi(G)-1.
\]

For the lower bound, the two nonadjacent twin vertices use order-sensitive rules obtained from an explicit sharply four-transitive permutation group on eleven points, followed by deletion and repair of one point. On each coordinate line the repaired rules are derangement permutations, are pointwise unequal, and have fixed-point-free composition. A Hall-matching argument then assigns consistent guesses to the four clique vertices.

## Structural additions

This disclosure also proves:

1. a general twin-completion/Hall criterion for attaining the upper endpoint `2n-2` on `K_n-e`;
2. an obstruction showing that, for even `n`, a twin rule that is set-symmetric and a permutation on every coordinate line cannot exist; and
3. a complete classification of the seven `M11` orbit labels under the deletion repair:

```text
repairable labels: 4,5,6,7,8,10
non-repairable:    9
compatible pairs:  {4,6}, {5,10}, {7,8}
```

The three compatible pairs form one orbit under the setwise stabilizer of the base tetrad. Label `9` is the unique Witt-design completion of that tetrad.

## Scope

This release proves the exact value for `K6-e` and the stated sufficient structural lemmas. It does **not** solve the full `K_n-e` family, characterize every winning upper-endpoint strategy, prove that order-sensitive twins are necessary outside the line-permutation framework, or claim journal peer review.
