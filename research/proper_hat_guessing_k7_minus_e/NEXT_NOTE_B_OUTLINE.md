# Draft outline — complete graphs minus one edge

> Working outline only. This is not a separate released theorem claim.

Provisional title:

> **Proper hat guessing on complete graphs minus an edge: completion designs and order-sensitive orbit maps**

Proposed spine:

1. General twin-completion/Hall theorem and exact coordinate-line criterion.
2. Disjoint completion-design theorem.
3. Even-`n` obstruction to set-symmetric line-permutation twins within the criterion.
4. Prime-admissibility theorem for `S(n-2,n-1,2n-2)` and Catalan block count.
5. `K5-e`: existing public Fano proof; align its independent-review standard or add explicit disjoint `S(3,4,8)` certificates before synthesis.
6. `K6-e`: reviewed punctured eleven-point sharply four-transitive construction and complete label classification.
7. `K7-e`: reviewed disjoint-Witt and twelve-point orbit constructions.
8. Taxonomy of symmetry: set-symmetric designs at odd `n`; necessarily order-sensitive line-permutation twins at even `n`; mixed symmetry at `n=7`.
9. Open directions: weaker packings, partially overlapping designs, and whether the orbit-map paradigm terminates after the Mathieu cases.

The paper should foreground the two construction paradigms and arithmetic boundary, not market itself as a single proof of every case through `n=7`.
